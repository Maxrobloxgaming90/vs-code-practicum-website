II. SALON IMPLEMENTATION SERVICES (Optional Add-On)

Services include:

  • Haircut aligned with face analysis
  • Hair color tone aligned with color analysis
  • Makeup application aligned with skin & color profile
  • Brow shaping aligned with face proportions

What they receive:

  • Immediate visible application of consultation results
  • Demonstration of correct technique
  • Explanation during application

They are paying for:

  • Professional execution of analyzed recommendations
  • Technique education during service

III. ONLINE CONSULTATION SERVICES (Limited Scope)

Online services are strictly educational and assessment-based.

Consultants review manually (no AI facial scanning).

Clients must submit:

  • Structured questionnaire
  • Self-taken photos under required lighting guidelines
  • Written concerns

What the client actually receives:

  • Simplified assessment summary
  • Ingredient guidance list
  • Basic routine structure
  • Recommendation to upgrade to in-person if deeper analysis is required

IV. MEMBERSHIP SERVICES

They are paying for:

  • Long-term tracking
  • Professional updates
